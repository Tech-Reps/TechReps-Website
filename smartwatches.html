Forensic Authenticity Checklist

Direct side-by-side comparison contrasting authentic hardware capabilities with known clone failure points.

GENUINE Clinical ECG & Multi-Wavelength PPG
Titanium crown galvanic electrical contacts wired to certified biosensing AFEs; optical array uses calibrated Green, Red, and Infrared LEDs with photodiodes for actual SpO2 and medical rhythm detection.
LACKING Fake BP/Glucose & Single Green LEDs
Blood pressure and glucose readouts use static calculation tables or random loops; optical sensors use 1–2 cheap green LEDs that frequently illuminate even when strapped to mugs or empty boxes.
GENUINE Dual-Frequency L1+L5 GPS & Magnetometer
Independent multi-constellation GPS locks in dense urban canyons; discrete 3-axis digital compass IC and genuine water depth/temperature transducers.
LACKING Phone-Tethered GPS & Simulated Compass
Most RTOS clones tether coordinates entirely from the smartphone's Bluetooth link; compass and depth gauge apps are static graphics or basic accelerometer estimations.
GENUINE Native watchOS & Secure Element NFC
Interactive 2-way notifications with full keyboard typing, native WatchKit app ecosystem, and hardware-encrypted embedded Secure Element (eSE) for Apple Pay and Google Wallet.
LACKING One-Way Push & Unencrypted Door RFID
Notifications are one-way Bluetooth pushes (via ANCS/notification listener) with no reply capability; onboard NFC is strictly an unencrypted 13.56MHz RFID transponder for electronic door locks.
GENUINE Calibrated Power & Wrist Ergonomics
36–72 hour runtime with low-power co-processors, seamless primary phone hand-off synchronization, and custom UI scaled precisely to round/square display curves.
LACKING Phone Silicon Drain & App UI Clipping
Standalone 4G Android clones cram quad-core smartphone chips (UNISOC SC8541E) into watch bodies, draining 700mAh in 4–8 hours with severe thermal wrist throttling and rectangular app clipping.

Portable Speaker Audits & Limitations

High-end brand speaker replicas struggle primarily with acoustic mass engineering, internal waterproof gaskets, and digital signal processing:

  • Supported: Basic Bluetooth 5.x pairing profiles, analog 3.5mm auxiliary inputs, basic illuminated RGB branding arrays.
  • Lacking: True IPX7 submersion waterproof seals (often missing rubberized membrane gaskets), high-excursion long-throw bass passive radiators, and official companion app OTA firmware updates.
