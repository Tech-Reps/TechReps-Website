Replica Naming Scheme

Our naming is read in three parts. Firstly, the letter (G, P, M) indicates the AirPods version (General, Pro, Max).
Secondly, the first number (1, 2, 3, 4...) represents the AirPods version (AirPods Pro 1, AirPods Pro 2...).
Thirdly, the last number (0, 1, 2...) represents the replica version. It often indicates the quality: higher is better.

Forensic Authenticity Checklist

Direct side-by-side comparison contrasting authentic hardware capabilities with known clone failure points.

GENUINE Apple Find My Mesh Network
Hardware-level U1/U2 Ultra-Wideband precision finding; broadcasts encrypted beacon signals even when buds are resting inside a closed charging case.
LACKING Fake Proximity Spoofing
No replica can connect to Apple's decentralized Find My mesh. Coordinates only refresh when active and paired via Bluetooth to your personal device.
GENUINE True MagSafe & Apple Watch Charging
Precision-aligned magnetic ring supporting genuine MagSafe chargers and inductive charging via Apple Watch puck chargers.
LACKING Weak Magnets & No Watch Charging
Replicas use weak generic case magnets that slide off vertical mounts; zero replicas support charging on an Apple Watch puck charger.
GENUINE Acoustic Equalization Vents
Acoustically tuned, porous micro-mesh vents that actively equalize ear canal pressure and feed outward noise-cancelling microphones.
LACKING Non-Porous Cosmetic Mockups
Side and top vents on clones are commonly non-perforated solid plastic decals, cosmetic paint, or non-porous mockups.
GENUINE Cryptographic Serial Number Pairing
Each earbud and charging case carries an individual, verifiable serial number tied to Apple cryptographic certificates.
LACKING Copied Public Serial Numbers
Clones copy valid serial numbers from real retail units; an Apple Coverage page showing a valid serial does NOT verify authenticity.
IMPORTANT NOTE: Due to the popularity of TigerBuilder (TB) units, there are many bad actors using the name TB to promote their Bluetrum/JL chipset units even though they are not officially licensed or made by TB (example: AirPods 4 / Pro 2 / Pro 3 1568F). Please be advised to check using the official Flycc app on iOS and Android to verify before purchase.

NOTE: Even though Huilian is the only unit that can connect to Apple devices via iCloud, it does not mean it's stable. Through several reports and user feedback, the unit was found to have connectivity issues as well as sensor sensitivity issues.
