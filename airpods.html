Replica Naming Scheme

Our naming is read in three parts. Firstly, the letter (G, P, M) indicates the AirPods version (General, Pro, Max).
Secondly, the first number (1, 2, 3, 4...) represents the AirPods version (AirPods Pro 1, AirPods Pro 2...).
Thirdly, the last number (0, 1, 2...) represents the replica version. It often indicates the quality: higher is better.

How to Spot a Clone: Side-by-Side Comparison

Quick checklist comparing genuine AirPods with replica versions so you can tell them apart instantly.

GENUINE Apple Find My Network & Precision Finding
True Find My tracking powered by Apple's U1/U2 chips. Can pinpoint exact location and track earbuds even when resting inside a closed charging case miles away.
LACKING Fake Find My (Bluetooth Only)
Replicas cannot connect to Apple's worldwide Find My mesh network. Location only updates when earbuds are actively connected to your phone via Bluetooth. If lost in a closed case, they cannot be tracked.
GENUINE Strong MagSafe & Apple Watch Charging
Features a strong built-in magnetic ring that snaps firmly onto MagSafe chargers/stands and supports inductive charging on Apple Watch puck chargers.
LACKING Weak Magnets & No Apple Watch Charger Support
Uses weak generic magnets that slide off vertical magnetic mounts. Crucially, zero replicas support charging on an Apple Watch puck charger—making this a quick physical test.
GENUINE Real Mesh Vents for Pressure & ANC
Acoustically tuned, porous micro-mesh vents that actively equalize ear canal pressure and allow external noise-cancelling microphones to function.
LACKING Cosmetic Black Decals / Painted Vents
Vents on most clones are solid plastic decals, black paint, or non-porous mockups with no real holes. They do not relieve ear pressure or assist with noise cancellation.
GENUINE Unique Individual Serial Numbers
The left bud, right bud, and charging case each carry distinct, individual serial numbers in iOS settings matching physical laser-etched prints.
LACKING Copied Serial Numbers (Fake Coverage Check)
Clones copy real serial numbers stolen from authentic retail boxes. Checking the serial on Apple's website will falsely show "Valid Coverage"—meaning a valid serial does NOT prove authenticity.
IMPORTANT NOTE: Due to the popularity of TigerBuilder (TB) units, there are many bad actors using the name TB to promote their Bluetrum/JL chipset units even though they are not officially licensed or made by TB (example: AirPods 4 / Pro 2 / Pro 3 1568F). Please be advised to check using the official Flycc app on iOS and Android to verify before purchase.

NOTE: Even though Huilian is the only unit that can connect to Apple devices via iCloud, it does not mean it's stable. Through several reports and user feedback, the unit was found to have connectivity issues as well as sensor sensitivity issues.
