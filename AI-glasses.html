How to Spot a Clone: Side-by-Side Comparison

Quick checklist comparing genuine Ray-Ban Meta glasses with replica versions so you can tell them apart instantly.

GENUINE Camera on Left Side
Authentic Ray-Ban Meta frames mount the camera lens on the wearer's left front corner and the recording LED on the right. The touch controls and capture button are on the right temple, additionally the right inner side has a light indications of recording, this light is only visible when recording.
LACKING Camera Flipped to Right Side (MT5 Series)
Both the MT5 Ultra (51mm) and MT5 Mini (49mm) flip only the camera lens to the wearer's right front corner (with the LED on the left). The touch controls stay on the right temple. This flipped camera position is the fastest visual giveaway, also on the right inner side there is no light indications of recording.
GENUINE 32 GB Built-In Storage
Comes standard with 32 GB of storage—enough for 100+ videos and 500+ photos. You can capture memories all day without needing to immediately transfer them to your phone.
LACKING 2 GB to 16 GB Storage (Varies by Seller)
Clones come with anywhere from 2 GB to 16 GB of storage depending on which seller you buy from. Basic 2 GB versions fill up after only a few video clips, forcing you to offload files frequently.
GENUINE 12 MP Ultra-Wide with HDR Processing
Custom 12 MP ultra-wide camera with high dynamic range (HDR), crisp detail, excellent low-light performance, and smooth video stabilization.
LACKING 8 MP Basic Camera
Uses a basic 8 MP sensor with a narrower view (alleged from Sony IMX 175, isn't verifiable though). Photos and videos lack HDR, have less detail, and get noticeably grainy in dim lighting.
GENUINE 5-Microphone 3D Spatial Audio
Advanced 5-microphone array captures immersive surround sound with intelligent wind-noise reduction so your voice comes through clearly outdoors.
LACKING Dual Mics for Voice Calls Only
Equipped with 2 basic voice microphones. Good enough for phone calls and language translation, but cannot record 3D spatial audio and struggles in windy conditions.
GENUINE Live Streaming & Meta AI Assistant
Stream hands-free directly to Instagram and Facebook, ask Meta AI questions about what you see, and read/send WhatsApp and Messenger messages by voice.
LACKING Wi-Fi Transfer Only (AiBuds App)
No direct social media streaming or built-in Meta AI. Photos and videos must be transferred to your phone via local Wi-Fi using the third-party AiBuds app.
GENUINE Premium Luxottica Acetate Frame
High-quality, durable acetate frame crafted by Luxottica with authentic branding, robust metal hinges, and precision optical prescription lens support.
LACKING Lightweight TR90 Plastic Frame
Made from molded TR90 plastic. While it is very light and comfortable (~35g vs. ~50g genuine), it feels lighter and less premium than authentic acetate.

Hardware Breakdown & Key Features

1. Lightweight All-Day Comfort (~35g vs. ~50g Genuine)

The MT5 frames are made from lightweight TR90 plastic and weigh only around 35 grams—roughly 30% lighter than authentic Ray-Ban Meta frames (~50 grams). If you wear glasses for hours at a time, this lighter weight dramatically reduces pressure on your nose bridge and behind your ears.

2. Rain & Dust Protection (IP65 Rating)

The MT5 series features genuine IP65 dust and water resistance with rubberized sealing around the camera lens and buttons. This means they are completely dust-tight and can handle heavy rain, sweat, and splashes, making them great for outdoor sports and cycling where genuine IPX4 frames might struggle.

3. Longer Video Recording (12-Minute Clips)

Many cheap clone glasses overheat and shut off after just 1 to 3 minutes. The MT5 series avoids this by using two separate processors: an Allwinner chip for camera video and a Bluetrum chip for Bluetooth audio. Splitting the workload keeps temperatures down and allows continuous 12-minute video clips with digital anti-shake.

4. Sizing: 51mm (Ultra) vs. 49mm (Mini)

The MT5 series comes in two frame widths: the MT5 Ultra (51mm) fits average-to-wider faces (similar to standard Ray-Ban Wayfarers), while the MT5 Mini (49mm) is scaled down for smaller or narrower faces. Both sizes share the exact same internal chips, camera, battery, and flipped left-side camera placement.

5. Internal Storage: 2 GB to 16 GB (Check Before Buying)

Internal memory is not the same across all sellers. Depending on which store you purchase from, listings include between 2 GB to 16 GB of built-in flash storage. The 2 GB version fills up quickly (after a few 12-minute clips) and requires regular offloading to your phone, while 8 GB and 16 GB models give you much more shooting headroom.

6. Battery Life & Fast-Charging Case

The built-in 300 mAh frame battery provides up to 11 hours of audio playback and standby. It comes with a 2000 mAh charging case that can recharge the glasses to full in about 40 minutes, giving you multiple full recharges on the go without needing a wall outlet.

7. Microphones & Real-Time Translation

Equipped with dual noise-cancelling microphones for clear voice calls. Using the free AiBuds companion app, you can use two-way real-time voice translation across 26 languages. Note that unlike genuine glasses, these cannot record 3D spatial surround sound.

Buyer's Summary & Honest Advice

  • What They Are: Close physical replicas of the Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer smart glasses. They look nearly identical from the outside, but run on an alternative dual-chip platform with the third-party AiBuds app rather than Meta AI, Facebook, or Instagram integration.
  • Available Sizes: 51mm (MT5 Ultra) for average or wider faces, and 49mm (MT5 Mini) for narrower face shapes. Both share identical internal hardware.
  • Best For: Anyone looking for an affordable pair of smart glasses for hands-free 12-minute video clips, podcasts and calls, lightweight 35g comfort, rain resistance, and travel translation.
  • Tradeoffs to Expect: The camera is flipped to the left side (instant visual giveaway); storage depends on the seller (2 GB to 16 GB); no direct social media live streaming; and camera/mic quality is meant for everyday utility rather than professional video production.
