1. Weight & Long-Session Ergonomics (vs. Authentic Ray-Ban Meta)

As a direct physical replica of the Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, the MT5 Ultra achieves an impressively featherweight 35-gram profile in TR90 polymer—substantially lighter than the 48.6g–50.8g authentic Ray-Ban Meta frames. For users sensitive to nose-bridge clamping and ear fatigue over 4+ hours, the ~30% reduction in mass makes a dramatic ergonomic difference for all-day wear.

2. Ingress Protection & Durability (IP65)

The MT5 Ultra boasts an IP65 rating utilizing internal adhesive sealing around optical camera modules and tactile physical buttons. It is completely dust-tight and immune to low-pressure water jets, making it genuinely resilient for cycling, commuting, and rainy outdoor fieldwork where IPX4-rated eyewear fails.

3. Dual-SoC & 12-Minute Thermal Envelope

Splitting video capture and audio onto dedicated silicon—Allwinner V821L2 for camera ISP/encoding and Bluetrum BT8961C5 for Bluetooth audio—curbs thermal buildup. This architecture enables sustained 12-minute continuous video clips per session with anti-shake, preventing the severe 1–3 minute thermal shutdowns seen on monolithic chips.

4. 2 GB SDNAND Storage Bottleneck

The MT5 Ultra’s most critical engineering bottleneck is its constrained 2 GB SDNAND flash storage. With multiple high-resolution video clips, internal memory fills rapidly, requiring users to regularly offload media to their phone over a direct Wi-Fi link to free up operational space.

5. Frame Battery & 2000 mAh Rapid Case

Temple-integrated 300 mAh cells deliver up to 11 hours of continuous audio streaming and standby operation. The system includes a 2000 mAh high-capacity charging case with rapid charge capability (~40 minutes to full), ensuring sustained off-grid endurance.

6. Dual ENC Voice & AiBuds Translation

Equipped with a dual Environmental Noise Cancellation (ENC) microphone array tuned for speech intelligibility. The AiBuds companion app drives face-to-face dialogue interpretation across 26 languages and real-time voice queries, prioritizing vocal clarity over spatial binaural recording.
