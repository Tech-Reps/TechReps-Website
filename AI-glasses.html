How to Spot a Clone: Side-by-Side Comparison

Quick checklist comparing genuine Ray-Ban Meta glasses with replica versions (MT5 Series & M02X Pro Max) so you can tell them apart instantly.

GENUINE Camera on Left Side
Authentic Ray-Ban Meta frames mount the camera lens on the wearer's left front corner and the recording LED on the right. The touch controls and capture button are on the right temple. Additionally, the right inner temple has an interior indicator light that shines when recording.
LACKING Camera Placement on Replicas (MT5 & M02X)
Both the MT5 series (Ultra 51mm / Mini 49mm) and M02X Pro Max put the camera lens on the wearer's right front corner. The touch controls remain on the right temple. This reversed camera position is the fastest giveaway, and replicas lack the inner recording light.
GENUINE 32 GB Built-In Storage
Comes with 32 GB of built-in storage (holds about 100 short videos and 500 photos). You don't have to offload clips to your phone right away.
LACKING 2 GB to 16 GB Storage (4 GB on M02X / 2–16 GB on MT5)
Clones have much less storage. Basic MT5 units start at 2 GB (fills up after two or three 12-minute clips), though some sellers offer 4 GB to 16 GB versions. The M02X Pro Max has 4 GB flash and creates its own local Wi-Fi hotspot to transfer videos directly to your phone.
GENUINE 12 MP Ultra-Wide with HDR Processing
Custom 12 MP ultra-wide camera with high dynamic range (HDR), crisp detail, excellent low-light performance, and smooth video stabilization.
LACKING 8 MP Sensors (Marketed as "13MP" / "4K")
Sellers often advertise these as "4K" or "13MP," but the actual sensors are 8 Megapixel (3280 × 2464 on the M02X Pro Max). The M02X Pro Max adds a 6-axis gyroscope for digital stabilization to reduce head bobbing, but footage still falls well short of real Ray-Ban Meta video in dynamic range and low light.
GENUINE 5-Microphone 3D Spatial Audio
Advanced 5-microphone array captures immersive surround sound with intelligent wind-noise reduction so your voice comes through clearly outdoors.
LACKING Dual Microphones with ENC (No Spatial Audio)
Both the M02X Pro Max and MT5 have 2 basic microphones with open-ear speakers. They work fine for quiet indoor phone calls, translation, and voice questions, but they pick up heavy wind noise outside and cannot record 3D spatial audio.
GENUINE Live Streaming & Meta AI Assistant
Stream hands-free directly to Instagram and Facebook, ask Meta AI questions about what you see, and read/send WhatsApp and Messenger messages by voice.
LACKING Third-Party AI Apps & Wi-Fi Sync (One AI Glass / AiBuds)
Replicas cannot live stream to Instagram or Facebook, and have no access to Meta AI. Instead, they use third-party apps: the M02X Pro Max uses the One AI Glass app (which uses ChatGPT for English/general queries and Qwen / 通义千问 for Chinese), while the MT5 Series uses the AiBuds app (basic translation in 26 languages).
GENUINE Premium Luxottica Acetate Frame
High-quality, durable acetate frame crafted by Luxottica with authentic branding, robust metal hinges, and precision optical prescription lens support.
LACKING TR90 Composite Plastic Frames (~35g–46g)
Replicas use lightweight molded TR90 plastic instead of acetate. The MT5 is noticeably light at ~35g, while the M02X Pro Max weighs ~46g with reinforced metal hinges. Both are comfortable to wear, but they feel like plastic sunglasses rather than premium eyewear.
GENUINE Multi-Layer Rigid Nesting Box
Real pairs come in a heavy, rigid multi-layer box with textured grey pull-out inserts and clean factory-printed labels.
LACKING Tiered Packaging & OEM Kits (MT5 & M02X)
MT5 clones come in three box styles depending on price: a $40 generic white box, a $50–$60 thin paper clone box, or a $100 multi-layer box with fake logos. The M02X Pro Max is not sold as a Ray-Ban replica; it comes in its own factory retail box with a 1400 mAh leather case, 3 swappable lenses (dark, clear blue-light, and transition), ear grips, and an instruction manual.

Internal Architecture & Hardware Details

1. Dual-Silicon Split

ARCHITECTURE

The MT5 Ultra uses two separate chips to save power. The Bluetrum BT8961C5 handles Bluetooth audio, phone calls, and temple touch controls. The Allwinner V821L2 video chip stays asleep until you take a photo or record a video clip.

2. Flipped Right-Temple Camera

CAMERA & SENSOR

Real Ray-Ban Meta frames have the camera on the left and the LED on the right. The MT5 flips this around and places the 8MP camera on the right front corner. It records up to 12 minutes per video file before saving.

3. Flash Storage & File Transfer

STORAGE & COMMS

Built-in storage ranges from 2 GB to 16 GB depending on what the seller orders from the factory. You can transfer clips through the AiBuds app or plug the glasses into a computer with USB-C to copy files directly.

4. Battery Life & Charging Case

POWER & BATTERY

Small batteries in each temple total 300 mAh for roughly 11 hours of audio playback or standby. The glasses charge through magnetic pins in the 2000 mAh faux-leather case and take about 40 minutes to recharge.

Buyer's Summary & Honest Advice

  • What to expect: These are budget camera glasses that copy Ray-Ban Meta styles or provide standalone POV recording. Both platforms run on a dual-chip setup: an Allwinner video processor paired with a Bluetrum or Jieli Bluetooth audio chip.
  • M02X Pro Max vs. MT5 Series:
    • M02X Pro Max: The better pick if you want to record video. It has a real 6-axis gyro for electronic stabilization, fast Wi-Fi transfer to your phone, 4 GB storage, a 1400 mAh leather case, and 3 sets of swappable lenses (sunglasses, blue-light clear, and photochromic). It is not a Ray-Ban replica, but its camera footage and accessories are noticeably better.
    • MT5 Series (Ultra 51mm / Mini 49mm): The pick if you want the classic Ray-Ban Wayfarer design. It weighs only 35g and comes in two sizes (51mm standard or 49mm small face). Video stabilization is basic digital without a gyro, storage varies by seller (2 GB to 16 GB), and it charges in a 2000 mAh Ray-Ban style case. (Note: The cat-eye MT6 Kylie Edition is an upcoming release, with hardware details to be announced).
  • Keep expectations in check: Real Ray-Ban Metas cost $300+ for a reason. Neither replica supports live streaming to Instagram or Facebook, and neither can connect to Meta View or Meta AI. Don't be fooled by listings claiming "4K" or "13MP"—both use basic 8MP sensors that get grainy in low light.
  • Bottom line: Buy the M02X Pro Max if you want usable POV video clips, easy phone file transfers, and extra lenses. Buy the MT5 if your main goal is having lightweight sunglasses that look like real Ray-Ban Metas.
